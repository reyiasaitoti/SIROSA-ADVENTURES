TRAVEL QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Details to check while you compare routes.

When is a good time to visit?

It depends on the parks and what you want to see. June to October is generally drier and busier; January and February are also popular. The Great Migration is usually in the Mara around July to October, but timing varies and wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

What does the quote include?

Ask for accommodation, meals, transfers, park fees and activities to be listed separately, along with exclusions and optional costs. What’s included can differ from one itinerary to another.

Are these fixed-date departures?

No. The routes on this page are samples. Share your dates and ask whether a private trip or a shared option is available for your route.

Can I bring children?

Often, yes. Share their ages when you enquire; camp age limits, room options and drive lengths can affect which route works best.
