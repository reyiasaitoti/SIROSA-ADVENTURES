KAJIADO COUNTY · SOUTHERN KENYA

Amboseli

Large elephant herds, open plains and wetlands beneath Mount Kilimanjaro. The mountain is often cloud-covered, so treat the view as a bonus, not a guarantee.

Home / Places / Amboseli
THE NATIONAL PARK

Elephants, wetlands and wide views.

Kenya Wildlife Service describes Amboseli as a mix of open grass plains, acacia woodland, bushland, swamps and marshes. The park is well known for elephant viewing and its view toward Kilimanjaro. Wet areas also attract birdlife; KWS lists more than 400 bird species.

Two or three nights makes a practical short visit from Nairobi. A full day in the park gives you more time for wildlife viewing than arriving late and leaving early the next morning.

WHAT TO SEE

Look beyond the skyline.

Amboseli’s habitats change across the park. Wildlife viewing often centres on the open plains and wetland areas, while Observation Hill gives visitors a raised view over part of the landscape.

  • Elephants: spend time with a guide and follow park rules around wildlife distances.
  • Wetlands and birds: ask your guide what habitats and bird activity are present during your visit.
  • Observation Hill: check current access and visitor guidance with KWS before your trip.
TRIP PLANNING

Stay close to your park days.

Choose accommodation based on the gate you’ll use, included meals and time needed for transfers. If you are combining Amboseli with another park, ask for realistic drive times rather than just a route map.

  • Short stay: plan at least one full day for game drives.
  • Photography: ask about early starts and the light expected for your dates.
  • Before booking: confirm park fees, vehicle arrangements and whether transfers are private or shared.
