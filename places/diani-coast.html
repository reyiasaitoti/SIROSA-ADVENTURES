THE SOUTH COAST

Finish the safari by the sea.

Diani is a beach destination on Kenya’s south coast, south of Mombasa. It works well as a separate beach stay or as the final part of a safari itinerary. The pace is different from a park visit: leave time for the beach, meals and transfers rather than packing every day with activities.

Swimming, snorkelling, diving and boat trips may be available through local operators. Conditions depend on tides, weather and operator schedules, so check what is running when you travel.

THINGS TO DO

Keep a little space in the day.

Diani can be as quiet or active as you like. Ask your hotel or a locally licensed operator which excursions are suitable for your dates and what is included in the price.

  • Beach time: check the tides and local swimming guidance before heading into the water.
  • On the water: ask about snorkelling, diving or boat trips, including equipment, transfers and weather policies.
  • Coastal excursions: confirm travel time, departure point and return arrangements before booking.
TRIP PLANNING

Plan the transfer as part of the trip.

After a safari, reaching the coast may involve a flight or a long road transfer. Compare the full journey, including airport transfers and connection times, before choosing.

  • From Nairobi: compare flight times and the transfer from the arrival airport to your accommodation.
  • From safari: check whether the itinerary uses a road transfer, airstrip connection or a combination.
  • At the coast: ask whether meals, beach access and activity transfers are included in your stay.
